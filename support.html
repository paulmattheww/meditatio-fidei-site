Cancel or manage Premium

Premium is $3.99/month or $29.99/year, auto-renewing through Apple. A free trial may be offered. To cancel:

  1. Open the iOS Settings app
  2. Tap your Apple ID name
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Tap Meditatio Fidei Premium
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription

You can also manage subscriptions at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

On-device AI model

The optional conversational model is about 2.3 GB and downloads over Wi-Fi from Settings. The app still works without it (prayers, library, calendar, and source-based answers).
