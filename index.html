PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

Developer: SOVEREIGN ACTION LLC ("we," "us," "our")

Summary

Meditatio Fidei is designed with privacy as a core principle. Prayers, chats, confession notes, and journal entries are stored on your device. We do not operate user accounts. Limited network requests are used for the liturgical calendar, optional AI model download, App Store purchases, and (only if configured) privacy-respecting analytics.

Data Collection

Meditatio Fidei does not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contact information
  • Browsing or search history
  • Content of prayers, journal entries, or conversations
  • Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) or any advertising identifiers

AI Model Provenance

Meditatio Fidei uses an on-device AI language model (Qwen3-1.7B-4bit) for generating theological responses. The model is downloaded once from Hugging Face during initial setup and thereafter runs entirely on your device using Apple's Metal GPU framework. No queries, prompts, or AI-generated responses are ever transmitted off your device. The model does not learn from or retain your conversations between sessions.

Corpus Data Provenance

The app includes a bundled SQLite database of 16,000+ text chunks drawn exclusively from public domain and freely available Catholic sources, including:

  • Douay-Rheims Bible (public domain)
  • Catechism of the Catholic Church (Vatican, freely distributed)
  • Butler's Lives of the Saints, Baring-Gould's Lives of the Saints, Pictorial Lives of the Saints (public domain)
  • 1913 Catholic Encyclopedia (public domain)
  • Summa Theologica by St. Thomas Aquinas (public domain)
  • Roman Martyrology, 1916 English edition (public domain)
  • Catechism of the Council of Trent, Baltimore Catechism (public domain)
  • Selected Church Fathers and patristic texts (public domain)
  • Marian apparition accounts and papal documents (public domain / freely distributed)
  • Moral theology references and Catholic prayers (public domain)

This corpus is stored locally and is never modified or transmitted.

On-Device Processing

All AI inference, text search, embedding generation, and data storage happen entirely on your iPhone or iPad. No queries, responses, or personal reflections are ever sent to external servers.

Analytics

Meditatio Fidei can use TelemetryDeck for privacy-respecting, aggregated usage analytics. TelemetryDeck does not collect personally identifiable information, IP addresses, or device fingerprints. Analytics are disabled unless a TelemetryDeck App ID is configured in a release build. When enabled, signals are used solely to understand which features are most useful. You can learn more at telemetrydeck.com/privacy.

Crash Reporting

The app includes a crash-reporting hook that can send anonymous technical reports (stack traces, device model, OS version) without conversation content, prayer journal entries, or screenshots. Crash reporting is not enabled in the current release (no DSN is configured).

App Tracking Transparency

Meditatio Fidei does not use the Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), does not participate in any ad networks, and does not track users across apps or websites. The app does not prompt for App Tracking Transparency permission because no tracking occurs.

Third-Party Services

  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co): Optional one-time download of the on-device AI model (~2.3 GB, Wi-Fi). Only standard HTTP requests are made; prompts and conversations are not uploaded.
  • LitCal / readings APIs: Used to fetch liturgical calendar data and Mass reading references. Requests include locale (EN), national calendar (USA), and calendar dates — not your identity or conversation content.
  • TelemetryDeck: Optional privacy-respecting analytics as described above (off unless configured).
  • Apple iCloud (optional): Preference keys only (reminder time, onboarding). Chats, prayers, confession notes, and journals stay on-device and are not synced.
  • Apple StoreKit: Subscription purchases are handled by Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage & Deletion

All user data (conversations, prayer sessions, journal entries, confession notes, discernment sessions) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. You can delete all local data at any time from the app's Settings screen. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data permanently.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Meditatio Fidei is not in the App Store Kids category. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The confession examination of conscience includes mature religious/moral questions (including sexual morality). We recommend an age rating of 12+ and parental guidance for younger users.

GDPR & CCPA

Because Meditatio Fidei does not collect, process, or store personal data on any server, users' rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) are inherently satisfied. There is no personal data to access, correct, delete, or port. If you have questions about your data rights, contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to: paul.washburn@sovereign-action.com
