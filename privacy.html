PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: September 12, 2026 · Developer: SOVEREIGN ACTION LLC · Bundle ID: com.paulmattheww.fides

Summary

Meditatio Fidei is designed with privacy as a core principle. Prayers, chats, confession notes, and journal entries are stored on your device. We do not operate user accounts. Limited network requests are used for the liturgical calendar, optional AI model download, App Store purchases, and (only if configured) privacy-respecting analytics.

What we do not collect

  • Name, email, or phone number (no account)
  • Location, contacts, or advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • The content of prayers, journal entries, confession notes, or AI conversations

On-device AI

Theological responses are generated on-device (Qwen3, Apple Intelligence when available, or local source excerpts). Prompts and responses are not uploaded. The optional model download (~2.3 GB) comes from Hugging Face over HTTPS on Wi-Fi; it does not include your questions.

Network requests

  • Liturgical calendar / readings: a calendar date is requested from public liturgical APIs. No account data is sent.
  • Hugging Face: optional one-time model download.
  • Apple StoreKit: purchases are processed by Apple. See Apple’s privacy policy.
  • TelemetryDeck: optional aggregated usage analytics, disabled unless an App ID is configured. No IP addresses or conversation content.
  • Crash reporting: not enabled in the current release.

App Tracking Transparency

Meditatio Fidei does not use IDFA, ad networks, or cross-app tracking, and does not request tracking permission.

Local storage & deletion

Conversations, prayer sessions, confession notes, and discernment sessions are stored with SwiftData on the device. Delete them from Settings → Delete All Local Data, or by uninstalling the app.

Not a Kids Category app

The confession examination of conscience includes mature religious and moral questions (including sexual morality). Recommended age rating is 12+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Purchases

Premium is $3.99/month or $29.99/year, auto-renewing through Apple, with a free trial when offered. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. See Support.
